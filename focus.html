What to focus on

Five actions based on the YouTube Studio data behind why this video brought the most subscribers.

1

Make more “learn AI by building” and “AI for your career” videos

Use the winning format: a named method, numbered steps and a live demo, 15–25 minutes long.

Why: the top subscriber videos are the learning method (11), job hunt (7), career shift (6) and no-code (5). Commentary videos get views but almost no subscribers.

2

Fix the first two minutes

Show the result first and cut the intro. Put the subscribe ask where viewers start to leave.

Why: this video averages about 1.6 minutes per view on a 17-minute video (about 10%). Viewers subscribe early and then drop off.

3

Stop using Shorts for subscriber growth

Use Shorts only to point viewers to a long video.

Why: around ten videos under a minute have 0 subscribers between them.

4

Build series: job hunt, CV, cover letters, career shift

Give every new viewer a clear next video to watch, and link each video to the next one in the series.

Why: 76% of viewers are new and only 7% are regular. CV (5 subs from 152 views) and cover letters (5 from 149) convert at about 3.3% each, above the 1.0% channel average, so they deserve more reach.

5

Track subscribers per 100 views, not total views

subscribers per 100 views = subscribers ÷ views × 100

Why: total views hide what works. The learning-method video has 2.9 per 100 views, LinkedIn Job Hunt 1.3 and the channel overall 1.0.
